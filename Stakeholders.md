# TF-Store-BA-Project1

Stakeholders Identification  
1. Users/ Customers- download, browse and shop on the app
2. TF Owners- The project sponsors. They care about return on investment (ROI), brand alignment, sales growth, and overall project cost.
3. Project manager- 
4.Storehouse staff- goods available, manages the shop, handle the physical inventory. The app must sync perfectly with their in-store stock levels to avoid selling a dress online that was just bought off the rack.
5. App management/ app Admin 
6. Payment gateway providers
Web dev- develops the app, , UI/UX designers, software engineers
7. Testers- test the working of the app
8. Packing/ shipping team - when a user placed an order it should be dispatched to the correct address, these staff ensure correct goods, properly packed is sent to correct address
9. Marketing/ Sales- advertising and making public know about the app. Keep track of the sales of TF
10. Customer service- all customer queries, feedback and complaints to be resolved. If not solved revert the same to BA, Pm Devs and testers of TF
11. Law regulators- all kinds of legal notices or actions taken care by them. Tax, privacy, customer rights and laws
12. 
13. Govt Regulations
14. Investor- roi, profit, growth, market share
15. TF employees- TF job security, fair play, a functional work environment
