List the requirements gathered from various stakeholders for the project

1. User requirement 

An App that's easy to navigate, safe to enter my personal and banking details. I should trust the app with payment as I am adding my banking details.
I should be able to view a dress's clear image from 4 sides, the exact color, size, material mentioned.
I should also be able to see how it looks on me in the simulation.
I want various Categorisation linke summer-winter collections, festival specials, discounted dresses etc.
A Proper Search Bar where I can enter my category of choice and it should show me relevant searches.
I would be happy to see price comparison with other apps.
I should be able to track my order. 
I should not receive wrong order. And should be easily able to return the wrongly shipped order if any.

2. Owner

A platform to showcase all dresses of TF to the customers. 
$40k, $10k 5 months
TF should be able to make a 10/% of the total TF's profit via the app.
In the 1st quarter achieve 20/% sales, 2/%conversion rates from downloads to purchases
In competition with existing online shopping apps, TF Store (app) should record download of 50k in the 1st month, 60k in the 2nd month and so on. By the end of 6 months aim for 5L downloads and 2L customer profiles to be created
Aim to engage customers on the app by 40 to 50 mins per week
Development must stay within the allocated budget
10% of website visitors should be purchasing via the app.
Sales Analytics Dashboard: Real-time reporting on revenue, conversion rates, average order value, and top-selling dresses.

3. Manager

2BA, 10 dev team, 10 testers, processor laptops, monitor to each.
Monthly meeting with BA and dev head, head of all teams and TF owner
Weekly evaluations

4. Storehouse staff
Absolute synchronization between the brick-and-mortar shelf stock and the app inventory to avoid overselling.

5. Website admin
Should be able to upload the product photo (5), description, price of each item.  Download reports regarding category wise sales, and other custom.
Should track each order.
Should have an update of the inventory

6. Payment gateway providers
Strict adherence to data security standards
Secure API Integration: Stable, encrypted communication tokens between the app frontend and their payment servers.
Fraud Prevention Data:

7. Dev/ Test team
Well-defined user stories and acceptance criteria before coding begins.
Scalable Architecture

8. Packing/ shipping team
We should get correct order details
Correct address of shipping
Clearance to ship
Packing materials (cardboard boxes, papers, thermocols, sheets etc)
The trucks in proper condition

9. Marketing/ Sales
Budget for ads
Sales report, most- least sold items
Ad approvals

10. Customer service
Integration showing a customer's entire order history, app activity, and support chat log on one screen.
A direct communication channel to help customers resolve sizing or checkout issues in real-time.
Customer service communication time should be 7am to 9pm, not late nights

11. Law regulators- 
Comply with mobile viewing standards
