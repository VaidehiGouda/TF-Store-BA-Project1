## Requirements Analysis

Build an app named TF store that helps the TF to showcase its dress and makes sales via the app. An expected 10%profit from this medium of sales.

The customer end
An attractive app with browsing options- The app must have an attractive homepage. There should be various categories to browse, a search bar. Categorise as men, women, kids, festive seasons, based on colors.
Each item described briefly- An apparel must have 5-7 photos, each in different angle, the name, color, size, materials used, price should be indicated just below the dress. All images should be actual none should be generated of copied from elsewhere. There should be purchase and add to cart option below these details.
Create user profile- To create the user profile should have compulsory and optional information. Compulsory information must include username, password, customer's name, phone, email, address with  pinned map location, whereas optional includes their favourites list, preferred category, color and size.
Purchase process- Once the user clicks buy option, it should take user to the next page that has details of the items to be purchased, if all the details are correct go to the next page that has all the saved addresses, choose the correct shipping address or choose a new address. After this details are correct  go to the next page, choose the payment method or add a new one. Choose a payment option. Go to the payments page, make payment, redirect back to the app. Show the details of purchased goods.
Notify for the purchased items- Send email notification of the shipped items with a real time order tracking link.
 
For the app
Secure and authorised database- The app should have a secure database to store the customer details. Only the authorised should have access.
Not a massive app- the app must not take too much of the mobile storage space or that gets stuck frequently.
Secure payment- A secure payment gateway should be interfaced not leading to any wrong transactions from either side.

On the admin end, 
Ease of adding product details- they should be able to add photos of the dress and add other details for that dress.
Download reports stats- They should be able to download various reports like sales, revenue and profit. They should be able to get the stats of number of browses, purchases etc.

On Customer service,
Proper communication from the customer- We should be able to receive customer query/ complain correctly. We should get proper communication in the form of email or call or complain via the app.
Query order details- We should get the customer order details to reply to their query.

The business,
10\%Profit and 20\%Sales- TF should be able to make a 10/% of the total TF's profit via the app. In the 1st quarter achieve 20/% sales, 2/%conversion rates from downloads to purchases
Number of downloads in 6 months- In competition with existing online shopping apps, TF Store (app) should record download of 50k in the 1st month, 60k in the 2nd month and so on. By the end of 6 months aim for 5L downloads and 2L customer profiles to be created
Time spent per week on the app- Aim to engage customers on the app by 40 to 50 mins per week
Stay within the budget- Development must stay within the allocated budget
